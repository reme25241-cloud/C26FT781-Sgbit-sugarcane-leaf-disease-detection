About CaneGuardAI

AI-powered sugarcane leaf disease detection & crop growth tracking, running locally in the field

CaneGuardAI helps farmers and field staff catch sugarcane leaf disease early and keep track of how each field is progressing through its growth cycle — all from a single dashboard that runs locally, with no dependency on cloud connectivity for diagnosis.

How It Works
Upload or Camera
Upload a photo, or capture a leaf live from your device camera
In-Browser Model
Classified client-side via TensorFlow.js — no image leaves the device
Diagnosis
Class, confidence score, and a treatment recommendation
Tracked & Analyzed
Logged to history and rolled up into dashboard trends
Dashboard & Analytics
  • Total, healthy, and diseased scan counts, plus average confidence
  • Weekly scan volume and disease-breakdown charts
  • Week-over-week growth rate for scan volume and disease rate
  • Outbreak alerts when disease rate crosses a threshold
  • Auto-generated suggestions based on current disease mix
  • Filterable, paginated scan history with CSV export
Crop Growth Tracking
  • Farmers log a field's planting date, variety, and area
  • Growth stage calculated automatically from days since planting
  • Five-stage cycle: Germination → Tillering → Grand Growth → Maturity → Harvest Ready
  • Stage-specific tips (fertilization, irrigation, pest watch)
  • Field-wise progress and crop-variety distribution charts
Tech Stack
  • Django (runs locally on Raspberry Pi)
  • Machine image model, classified client-side via TensorFlow.js
  • Browser file upload and live camera capture (getUserMedia)
  • Bootstrap 5 + Chart.js frontend
  • SQLite for scan history, thumbnails, and tracked fields
Detected Classes
  • HealthyNormal leaf, no treatment needed
  • SmutWhip-like black fungal growth
  • Fungal DiseaseGeneral fungal infection
  • Grassy ShootExcessive thin tillering, spread by leafhoppers
  • MosaicViral mottling pattern
  • RustOrange pustules on leaf surface
  • Leaf ScaldFungal streaking, wilting
  • BorerStem/leaf boring insect damage
  • PestsGeneral pest damage, unspecified
  • Red RotFungal stalk infection
